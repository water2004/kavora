The directional planet, rings, accretion flow and stellar environment are
adapted into the current renderer's Vulkan dome compositor. Background
generation is cached, and the composed environment supplies camera,
reflection, refraction and illumination rays. Animation uses the host world
clock. The reference's procedural noise is used without external textures.
Its separate display-fog pass and renderer framework are not incorporated.
