AutoPbr.java adapts the material classification, luminance-derived normals,
and roughness variation from CE to Minecraft 26.3 and the current atlas owner.
Authored channels are preserved. Animated sprites are excluded, and normal
synthesis uses the DirectX convention of the native texture pipeline without
allocating an intermediate height array. Generation runs only on atlas rebuild.

The LabPBR converter also uses the perceptual roughness encoding documented
and implemented in CE. Remix itself performs the perceptual-to-linear square.
The selectable legacy double-squared encoding and CE's wet-surface system
are not included.

The lighting rules adapt CE's exposed-face area lights, lantern emitters,
end-rod geometry, and torch-relative power calibration. Minecraft 26.3 state
families, glow berries, and waterlogged sea pickles are integrated with the
current Sodium capture and render-thread light lifecycle.
