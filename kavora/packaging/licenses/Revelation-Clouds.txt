Kavora ports the cloud density from shaders/lib/atmosphere/clouds/Shape.glsl
and adapts the short light-ray integration from shaders/lib/atmosphere/clouds/Render.glsl
to its world origin, texture bindings and Vulkan volumetric integrator.
The source texture channels are stored at their original resolutions and
are repacked for Vulkan:

  shaders/texture/Noise2D.png
  shaders/texture/cloud/AlligatorFbm_128.bin
  shaders/texture/cloud/SimplexGrad_64.bin
  shaders/texture/cloud/CurlNoise.png
  shaders/texture/cloud/CirrusLutRev.png
  shaders/texture/cloud/HaringPro/CloudMapLow.bin
  shaders/texture/cloud/HaringPro/CloudMapHigh.bin
  shaders/texture/cloud/HaringPro/vertical_gradient_lut_32x32.bin
