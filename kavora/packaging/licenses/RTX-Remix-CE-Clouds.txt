Adapted from CloudNoise.java and CloudField.java to the native Vulkan cloud
pipeline. The periodic base-shape, erosion and weather fields are generated
on the GPU once per device. All scene rays consume the same density.
The OpenGL compositor, CPU near-cloud meshes, occupancy and history systems
are not incorporated. Self-shadow rays retain the complete density field,
including erosion, rather than Community Edition's base-only approximation.
The current runtime's ray-segment and multiple-scattering integrators remain.
