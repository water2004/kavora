Lunar color and elevation map
============================

NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (Ernie Wright).
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

Color: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Wide Angle Camera mosaic.
Elevation: Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA).
Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio and the LRO instrument teams.

The 2048 x 1024 RGB/elevation PNG is supplied by Caustica-Horizon,
revision ea3dc4bf67fc3bfce1860dbb025c71626b26e983:
src/main/resources/assets/caustica/textures/environment/moon_rgbh.png
https://github.com/West68/Caustica-Horizon
RGB contains lunar color and alpha contains LOLA elevation. The file is
included unchanged, with SHA-256:
281972c3eb6c12302764b1edb4d283bcfe650879cdf89ad5ee238eeafbf823aa

NASA SVS content is in the public domain unless otherwise noted:
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/
NASA media usage guidelines:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
